## Research Question

For roadmap M-2's S-03, "a product's page looks shops up and shows decisions through the guardian, with the same results" (`context/foundation/roadmap.md:109-122`), this research answers eight questions:

1. How does the product page (`src/pages/watchlist/[id].astro`) work today: how it loads the watched product and its decisions, how it decides each matched shop's step, how it runs the lookups and what each lookup writes over which stored decision, and how it builds the island's props and the price keys?
2. Where is a product's matched shops derived (`matchedShopsOf` and any equivalent comparison), in production code and in tests?
3. Where does the reading rule "an unreadable shop first, then that shop's decision" live, and which callers see which precedence when the product's read and its decisions' read disagree?
4. Where is "a lookup never overwrites a settled decision" enforced, by what, and can any path still overwrite one?
5. What does „Do sprawdzenia” hold in code, against the PRD and the glossary (D-01), and which PRD lines need an update?
6. What could load once (a `WatchedProduct`), which consumers would take it, which of them are browser-bundled, and must `watched-product.ts` join `islandConfig`?
7. Which tests pin what the user sees on this path?
8. Which technical debt and risks does this path carry?

Inputs read in full: the roadmap (S-01, S-03), `context/domain/02-invariant-aggregate-refactor.md`, `context/domain/domain-distillation.md`, `context/domain/glossary.md`, `context/map/repo-map.md` and its four evidence files, `context/foundation/lessons.md`, S-01's `research.md`, `plan.md` and `reviews/impl-review.md`, and `CLAUDE.md`'s UI, Shops and matching, and Data paragraphs (`CLAUDE.md:58-60`). No shop, Supabase, server or browser was asked anything.

## Summary

- **The page is the one production caller of the step runner and of the price builder, and it composes them in its frontmatter** (`runMatchSteps` and `productPricesOf` have no other production caller, V12). It reads the watched product and its decisions at once (`src/pages/watchlist/[id].astro:53-60`), derives the matched shops itself (`:78`), runs one step per matched shop (`:92-105`; `src/lib/services/shop-matching.ts:312-348`), then builds the island's price keys from the steps' items (`:115-116`; `src/lib/services/prices.ts:312-325`). No unit test imports the page; its composition is copied by hand into the seam table's `productPage` helper (`src/lib/services/price-pages.test.ts:185-226`).
- **A lookup writes without asking the guardian.** `lookupOutcome` calls `recordLookup` directly (`src/lib/services/shop-matching.ts:385`). "Never over a settled decision" is enforced twice, in other forms: `decideMatchStep` looks a shop up only without a decision, or over a stored "not found" on a retry (`src/lib/services/match-step.ts:64-77`), and `record`'s update narrows a lookup's write to `state = 'not_found'` (`src/lib/services/matches.ts:326-328`). No app path overwrites a match or a decline; a direct PostgREST update by the user still can, the accepted risk of `CLAUDE.md:60`.
- **Matched shops are derived in 8 production calls of `matchedShopsOf` in 7 files at HEAD**, one more than the domain map counted at f798c04: S-01 added `src/lib/services/watched-product.ts:68`. Three more production comparisons against the product's `source` pick the own shop on their own (`src/lib/services/price-comparison.ts:599`, `src/lib/services/price-targets.ts:92`, `src/lib/services/prices.ts:323`). On one product page view, the matched shops are derived twice on the page's own path (`[id].astro:78` and, through `productPricesOf`, `price-comparison.ts:603`).
- **One reading rule in five copies; one pair of reads with three different answers.** "A shop listed unreadable is unreadable; otherwise its stored decision stands" is read in `standingFrom` (`watched-product.ts:75-82`), `decideMatchStep` (`match-step.ts:61-64`), `itemInRows` (`price-targets.ts:96-100`) and, as sets, `productTargets` (`price-targets.ts:165-168`); the list reads its own read the other way round (`src/lib/services/watchlist-rows.ts:444-451`). The product and its decisions are read in 4 places with different answers when they disagree; most notably, **the page renders a product whose decisions couldn't be read at all, while `loadWatchedProduct` answers `failed`** (`[id].astro:68-72` with `match-step.ts:61-62`, against `matches.ts:487`). So the page can't take `loadWatchedProduct` as it is without changing what the user sees.
- **`WatchedProduct` holds too little for the page.** It is `{ itemId, ownShop, standings }` (`watched-product.ts:23-27`), without the product's own fields, its own item's id or price keys, while the page needs the `WatchlistProduct` for its title, lookups and links. Every consumer that would take a loaded product is server-side, so `watched-product.ts` can stay out of `islandConfig` as long as the list keeps `matchedShopsOf` in the browser-safe `price-comparison.ts` (`eslint.config.js:94-146`), which the roadmap's Parked list keeps ("One guardian per product on the list", `context/foundation/roadmap.md:147`).
- **D-01 is a gap in the PRD, not in the code.** The code's „Do sprawdzenia” also holds a product any of whose prices isn't fresh: stale, its item missing, never checked, or its row unreadable (`watchlist-rows.ts:241-243`), by "the owner's rule" of `etykiety-redesign` (`context/archive/2026-09-30-etykiety-redesign/plan.md:501-506`). The glossary already says so (`context/domain/glossary.md:45`). The PRD's one defining sentence lists only matching states (`context/foundation/prd.md:136`), and the PRD's convention is a new dated update, not an edit.
- **Proof of "nothing moved":** 10 e2e specs (9 open a product page), the route tests, the seam table and the service suites pin it. Some unit tests arrange internal shapes (`decideMatchStep({ matches })`, `runMatchSteps({ matches, shops })`), so "assertions unchanged" holds without editing them only if those signatures stay or are wrapped (Open Questions 1).
- **S-01's review finding F5 is carried to S-03 by the owner's triage**: the read side goes through `watchedProductOf` and `loadWatchedProduct`, with one precedence for a missing product against a failed read. The coordinating agent relayed that triage on 2026-10-10; at 3df527b the review file still reads PENDING (`context/changes/decision-route-guardian/reviews/impl-review.md:94-105`). This research confirms F5's facts, finds a fourth reader of the pair (`itemInRows`), and adds the page's third outcome, which that one precedence must leave as it is (Open Questions 2).
- **The guardian's refusals, as S-01's branch has them after the F1 fix the owner ordered** (relayed on 2026-10-10, not visible at 3df527b): a confirm of the item of the user's own match is an `outdated-form` (`decided=1`), no longer an `illegal-move`, which stays only for a decline over the user's decline (§4).

## Detailed Findings

### 1. How the product page works today

#### 1.1 Loading the watched product and its decisions

- The id must parse as a UUID (`[id].astro:53`); the product and its decisions are read at once, `getWatchlistProduct` and `listMatches` with its default, every priced shop (`:54-60`; `src/lib/services/matches.ts:448-470`). Without a Supabase client the product reads as `failed` (`:60`).
- At the same time, the list beside the product makes the list page's own three reads, `listWatchlist`, `listMatchStates` and `listLatestPrices` (`:61-67`). So one view reads the product's decisions twice, in two column sets: the page's `COLUMNS` (`matches.ts:346-348`) and the list's narrower select (`matches.ts:542`).
- The status comes from the product's read alone: null is 404 and `failed` is 503 (`:68-72`). A product whose decisions couldn't be read at all still renders: `matches` is null, and every matched shop's step becomes `read-failed` (`match-step.ts:61-62`).
- The page derives its matched shops once, `matchedShopsOf(shown.source)` (`:78`), and gives them to 5 consumers: `retryShopOf` and `repinShopOf` (`:82-83`), `runMatchSteps` (`:103`), and `decisionNotice` and `decisionError` (`:132-133`). The four that read the address narrow its shop to one of those through `parseMatchedShop` (`price-comparison.ts:71-76`; `match-step.ts:23-34`; `src/lib/services/match-view.ts:407-438`).

#### 1.2 Deciding each matched shop's step

`decideMatchStep` (`match-step.ts:60-78`) runs once per matched shop, called only from `runStep` (`shop-matching.ts:320`). Its checks, in order:

1. Decisions not read at all, or the shop listed unreadable: `read-failed` (`:61-63`).
2. A stored match or decline (`isRepinnable`, `:81-83`): `repin` when `?repin=` names this shop on the user's own navigation, else `stored` (`:64-67`).
3. A stored "not found" is `stored` unless `?retry=` names this shop (`:68-71`).
4. No decision, or a "not found" being retried: `prompt` when the request isn't the user's own navigation or the page was opened to re-pin or retry another shop (`:73-76`).
5. Otherwise `lookup`, with `retry` set for a retried "not found" (`:77`).

`outcomeOf` maps the kinds (`shop-matching.ts:331-348`): `read-failed`, `stored` and `prompt` only build a view; `repin` runs the shop's searches and stores nothing (`:356-368`); `lookup` runs `lookupOutcome` (`:375-416`). `autoRefreshOf` lets the island refetch on its own only on the user's own navigation with no `repin` step (`match-step.ts:90-92`; `[id].astro:119-122`).

#### 1.3 Running the lookups, and what each writes

- `runMatchSteps` runs every given shop at once, `shops ?? matchedShopsOf(input.product.source)` (`shop-matching.ts:312-315`); the page always passes `shops` (`[id].astro:103`), so the default runs only in tests. A step that throws shows its shop as giving no answer (the view `unavailable`) and stores nothing more (`:318-328`).
- A lookup's searches (`lookupInShop`, `:71-110`): an EAN search when the shop's adapter searches by EAN and the product has a usable EAN, then one name search only when the EAN search's pick found nothing or no EAN search ran; Rossmann and Super-Pharm skip the EAN search (`searchesByEan` false, `CLAUDE.md:59`). So at most 2 requests per matched shop per view.
- What it writes, by outcome (`:380-415`):

| lookup outcome                         | write                                                                                                                                                                            | over which stored decision                                                                                               | page shows                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `accepted`                             | `recordLookup`: `matched`, `decided_by` `auto`, the item's columns (`matches.ts:259-270`); if saved and the candidate has an offer, one price check (`shop-matching.ts:387-393`) | the insert when none is stored (`matches.ts:304-307`); on 23505, an update only where `state = 'not_found'` (`:321-328`) | the match's card (`shop-matching.ts:404-407`)        |
| `not-found`                            | `recordLookup`: `not_found`, `auto`, no item (`matches.ts:268`)                                                                                                                  | the same insert, then update narrowed to `not_found`                                                                     | "not found" (`shop-matching.ts:408`)                 |
| `choose`                               | nothing                                                                                                                                                                          | —                                                                                                                        | the choice, its forms below the island (`:410-411`)  |
| `unavailable`, the shop gave no answer | nothing                                                                                                                                                                          | —                                                                                                                        | why the shop gave no answer (`:412-414`, `:419-421`) |

- The write's results (`matches.ts:297-343`): `saved`; `decided` when the update finds no row, because a match or a decline was stored meanwhile (`:342`), which the page shows as `decidedView` (`shop-matching.ts:400-403`); `gone` on 23503 (`matches.ts:312-314`); `failed` on any other insert error, a failed update or an update answer that isn't a list (`:316-319`, `:333-341`). `failed` and `gone` mark the step `unsaved`, so the next view looks the shop up again (`shop-matching.ts:396`). A retry that saved sets `retried`, and the page redirects to its plain address (`:398`; `[id].astro:106-109`).

#### 1.4 The island's props and the price keys

- **Price keys.** `productPricesOf(supabase, shown, steps)` (`[id].astro:115-116`; `prices.ts:312-325`) takes each step's `item`, a stored match's or the match a lookup just stored, and calls `productPriceKeys` with its default shops (`prices.ts:318-321`). That puts the own item first (`price-comparison.ts:599-602`), then each matched shop's match (`:603-609`). One read of `price_summaries` follows (`prices.ts:175-176`, `:255-276`). `productPricesOf` then hands `priceShopsOf` each shop's link, the product's own page for the own item and the item's page for a match, by comparing each shop with `product.source` (`prices.ts:322-324`).
- **The island's props** (`[id].astro:239-256`): `itemId`, `listFilter`, the product's display fields, `matched` (one `MatchedShopView` per step, a choice's candidates stripped, notice and error by shop, `:137-143`), `shops` (the priced shops above), `autoRefresh`, `now` and `pricesFailed`. A shop's choice renders below the island, in `MatchChoice` (`:146-149`, `:259-261`).
- **The island's props name no own shop** (`[id].astro:239-256`). It draws a priced row without a match card as the plain `ShopCard` (`src/components/watchlist/PriceComparisonView.tsx:156-186`), so the own shop is implied by the props.
- **Later price requests re-derive the item from the rows.** The island's refetch posts the item it shows; `priceTargetFor` reads the product, takes its own item when the shop is its own (`price-targets.ts:92-94`), else reads its decisions (`:95-100`), and answers `changed` (409) for another item (`:70`), the guard against a page left open after a re-pin elsewhere (`CLAUDE.md:58`). The product's "Odśwież ceny" without JavaScript builds its keys from the stored decisions, not the page's steps (`productTargets`, `price-targets.ts:150-170`).
- **The selected row.** Its tag is recomputed from the page's own reads (`selectedRowTagOf`, `[id].astro:169-170`), while its line and the chips' counts come from the list's reads made before the page's lookups (`:61-67`, `:171-177`).

### 3. The reading rule's copies, and the precedences

**The rule.** "A shop whose decision couldn't be read is unreadable, never undecided; a decision that was read stands" (`matches.ts:384-400`; lesson "Never read an unreadable answer as missing", `context/foundation/lessons.md:19-24`).

| where                                        | form                                                                                                                    | read it consumes            |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `listMatches`, `matches.ts:466-469`          | producer: a shop is unreadable only when an odd row may be its decision and no decision of it was read                  | one product's `MatchesRead` |
| `standingFrom`, `watched-product.ts:75-82`   | `read.unreadable.includes(shop)` first, then the shop's decision                                                        | `MatchesRead`               |
| `decideMatchStep`, `match-step.ts:61-64`     | the same, plus a null read                                                                                              | `MatchesRead \| null`       |
| `itemInRows`, `price-targets.ts:96-100`      | the same, plus a null read                                                                                              | `MatchesRead \| null`       |
| `productTargets`, `price-targets.ts:165-168` | as sets: keys from `read.matches`, unread from `read.unreadable` narrowed to the matched shops                          | `MatchesRead`               |
| `matchStateIn`, `watchlist-rows.ts:439-459`  | the list's own producer and consumer: a decision that was read first, then the product's odd row or an unattributed one | the list's `DecisionsRead`  |

Because `listMatches` never lists a shop with a decision as unreadable (`matches.ts:469`), "unreadable first" and "decision first" give the same answer for its readers. The list's read keeps odd rows apart from decisions (`matches.ts:552-572`), so its order matters there, and it reads decision first (`watchlist-rows.ts:447-451`).

**The precedences.** The pair "the product and its decisions" is read in 4 places: at once in the page (`[id].astro:59`), in `loadWatchedProduct` (`matches.ts:483`) and in `productTargets` (`price-targets.ts:155-158`); one after the other in `itemInRows` (`price-targets.ts:85`, `:95`). What each answers:

| case                                          | the page (`[id].astro:68-72`, `match-step.ts:61-62`) | `loadWatchedProduct` → decision route (`matches.ts:484-487`; route `:55-61`) | `itemInRows` → `/api/watchlist/prices` (`price-targets.ts:85-100`) | `productTargets` → `/api/watchlist/refresh` (`price-targets.ts:159-168`) |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| product not on the list, decisions unreadable | 404                                                  | `gone`                                                                       | `gone` (404); decisions never read                                 | **`failed`** (`?prices=failed`)                                          |
| product not on the list, decisions read       | 404                                                  | `gone`                                                                       | `gone` (404)                                                       | no keys (`?prices=none`)                                                 |
| product's read failed                         | 503                                                  | `failed`                                                                     | `failed` (503)                                                     | `failed`                                                                 |
| product read, decisions unreadable at all     | **renders**, every matched shop `read-failed`        | `failed`                                                                     | own item served; a matched shop `failed`                           | `failed`                                                                 |
| product read, one shop's decision odd         | that shop `read-failed`                              | that shop's standing `unreadable` → `error=failed`                           | that shop `failed`                                                 | that shop unread, the others refreshed                                   |

- **So the callers see:** "gone first" in the page, the decision route and the prices route; "failed first" in the product refresh (F5's finding, `impl-review.md:100-103`). F5 named 3 places; `itemInRows` is a fourth, gone-first by sequence. The owner carried F5 to S-03 with one precedence (relayed, see the Summary); since the read side is to go through `loadWatchedProduct`, that is its own, the product's read first (inference), and only `productTargets` changes, in the table's first row above.
- **A third outcome F5 didn't name:** with the product read and its decisions unreadable at all, the page renders, while the other three answer `failed` for every matched shop.
- **Privacy is unaffected in every column:** under RLS another user's product reads as null, so it answers like a missing id (`src/lib/services/watchlist.ts:228-263`; `scripts/check-two-users.mjs:222-279`).

### 4. "A lookup never overwrites a settled decision"

**Where it's enforced today, by what:**

- **At read time, by `decideMatchStep`:** a stored match or decline is only shown or re-pinned; a stored "not found" is looked up again only on `?retry=` (`match-step.ts:64-71`).
- **At write time, by the compare-and-swap in `record`:** a lookup passes no `replaces` (`matches.ts:269`), so its update runs only where the stored state is `not_found` (`:326-328`), and a match or a decline stored meanwhile answers `decided` (`:342`). Pinned by `src/lib/services/matches.test.ts:871-881` (the narrowed update) and, against the real database, `src/lib/services/matches.db.test.ts:136-147` (a lookup that started before the user's pick stores after it: `decided`, the pick stands).
- **Not by the guardian.** `admitDecision` judges only a confirm or a decline from a posted form (`watched-product.ts:100-120`); no lookup admission exists at HEAD. The domain plan's `lookUp` refuses a settled standing as `settled` (`context/domain/02-invariant-aggregate-refactor.md:163-170`, `:196-203`).
- **The guardian's refusals and their codes**, as the decision route maps them (`src/pages/api/watchlist/matches.ts:18-23`), after the F1 fix:
  - `not-a-matched-shop`, the own shop included: `error=invalid`, shown on no card for the own shop;
  - `unreadable`: `error=failed`;
  - `outdated-form`: `decided=1`, "Ten produkt ma już zapisaną decyzję.", for a form whose `replaces` doesn't name the stored decision and, after F1, also for a confirm of the item of the user's own match;
  - `illegal-move`: `error=invalid`, after F1 only for a decline over the user's decline.
  - At 3df527b, `isLegalMove` still refuses the repeated confirm as `illegal-move` (`watched-product.ts:141-148`). S-01's review found that a page's own outdated form can send it (F1, `impl-review.md:36-49`); the owner had it fixed on S-01's branch (relayed on 2026-10-10). A lookup admission would sit beside these, with a settled standing answered as a decision already stored.
- **Not by the database.** RLS lets the user update their own decision in any state (`supabase/migrations/20261001182905_watchlist_removal_and_repin.sql:24-29`, read with grep), and the update grant covers the decision's columns (`supabase/migrations/20260928011450_price_observations.sql:27-31`, grep).

**Paths that write `watchlist_matches` in production code: one.** `record` (`matches.ts:297-343`), reached from `recordLookup`, whose only caller is `lookupOutcome` (`shop-matching.ts:385`), and from `recordDecision`, whose only caller is the decision route (`src/pages/api/watchlist/matches.ts:69`). The re-pin step stores nothing (`shop-matching.ts:356-368`), and a refresh writes price checks only (`src/lib/services/price-refresh.ts:65-92` → `prices.ts:81-98`), so no refresh un-pins a match (I-6, R-06).

**Can a path still overwrite one?**

- **A race, through the app: no** for a match or a decline. The narrowed update answers `decided`.
- **A "not found" overwritten by another view's lookup: yes, by design.** Two views that both read no decision can both look up; the second write lands over the first's `not_found` (inference from `matches.ts:326-328`). "Not found" isn't settled: the domain plan treats it like undecided (`02-invariant-aggregate-refactor.md:166`).
- **A retry over a decision that changed meanwhile: no.** The retry's update is the same narrowed one, `decided` if a match or decline landed first.
- **The product's own shop: no lookup runs there**, since steps run in the matched shops only (`shop-matching.ts:303-315`).
- **A direct PostgREST update by the user: yes**, for their own decisions only, the accepted risk ("A direct call can still set a user's own decision back to `not_found`", `CLAUDE.md:60`; roadmap Parked, `context/foundation/roadmap.md:146`).
- **Test gap:** no `runMatchSteps` test drives a lookup whose write answers `decided`, `failed` or `gone` (search of `src/lib/services/shop-matching.test.ts` for a `decided` view and for 23503 found none). The page's `decidedView` on that path (`shop-matching.ts:400-403`) is unpinned. The store's side is pinned in `matches.db.test.ts`, which `npm run test` leaves out (`vitest.config.ts:14`) and CI's `smoke` job runs with `npm run test:db`.

### 5. „Do sprawdzenia”: code, PRD and glossary

**The code** (`listRowOf`, `watchlist-rows.ts:241-243`; its own words at `:213-216`) holds a product when either holds:

- **a matched shop's state** (`needsCheck`, `:186-202`): no decision, a choice still to be made included (`none`); "not found"; a decision that couldn't be read; or an automatic match whose size or brand definitely differs (`matchStateIn`, `:452-458`). A decline doesn't count, nor does a match the user confirmed.

## Architecture Insights

- **Two guards for one rule, neither of them the guardian.** "Settled stays settled" is decided at read time by `decideMatchStep` and checked at write time by `record`'s compare-and-swap; S-01 put the same two-layer shape around user decisions (the guardian checks the form against the decision just read, and the compare-and-swap checks it again, `watched-product.ts:97-98`). Extending it to lookups keeps that shape.
- **"Each reader narrows" was a deliberate call.** `add-from-other-shops` chose no shared helper: both reads take every priced shop, "and each reader narrows per product" (`context/archive/2026-10-06-add-from-other-shops/plan.md:637`). S-03 reverses that for the per-product readers only; the list keeps its own.
- **The per-product view and the list view are two models.** The page reads one product's full decisions (`COLUMNS`); the list reads every product's states in a narrower column set and keeps odd rows apart (`DecisionsRead`). Their reading rules agree in effect but not in order (§3), and the roadmap keeps them apart.
- **Failures are results, not exceptions,** across every service read here (`RecordResult`, `MatchesRead | null`, `"failed"`), the idiom S-01 kept for the guardian (`context/changes/decision-route-guardian/plan.md:63`).
