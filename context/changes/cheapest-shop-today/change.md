## Notes

Roadmap S-03, the north star: see which shop is cheapest today, on the watchlist and on each product. The watchlist shows each product's cheapest shop and its price, with the price's age, so the owner knows where to buy without opening the product (the owner's request, 2026-09-28). A product's page shows its matched shops (Rossmann and Natura) ordered by today's price with the cheapest marked: regular and promo price, the Omnibus 30-day low, the source and age of every price, online prices labelled as online, prices appearing shop by shop, and a failed shop showing its last known price with its age or a clear gap.
