# Domain distillation: Drogeria Radar

## Summary

Drogeria Radar's domain is one shopper's repeat purchases. A product they buy again and again is found once in each of four Polish drugstore chains; from then on the app names the shop that sells it cheapest today, shows every price as an online price with its age, and says whether today's price is a good one, against the product's own price history or the shop's declared 30-day low.

Two subdomains are Core: the cheapest-shop comparison with its good-price judgement, and cross-shop product matching, whose confirmed per-shop item the PRD makes the product's anchor. The table holds 71 Core and Supporting terms (45 Core, 26 Supporting). 4 are missing in code, and the owner's own decisions deferred or dropped all of them: the daily refresh, manual price entry, the price per unit and the adapter health page. 5 more are named only in comments, labels or database policies (gap, anchor, watcher, online price, circumvention).

No documented rule is contradicted or ignored. Privacy, append-only prices and the shop cap are guarded in the database, and matching and pricing in pure, tested modules. Every hole the code leaves open is an accepted risk the documents record: a watcher's fake price, a shop stopped by a direct call, a user rewriting their own decisions.

The strongest drift is „Do sprawdzenia” (D-01). The PRD's newest definition lists only matching states, while the code, as the hebe-in-comparison plan decided, also holds every product whose price isn't fresh. #1 is the watched product with its shop decisions: it carries the anchor of every comparison, and its 18 rules are spread over five layers. One of them, the own-shop rule, is re-derived in eight places on read and isn't checked on write.

Drift by fix side: code 0, docs 1, decision 1. Open questions: 5.

### Code-only rules

- A Rossmann answer that names another product id is never stored as this product's price (`src/lib/services/shops/rossmann.ts:282-286`). _Searched in docs_ (`CLAUDE.md`, `context/foundation/test-plan.md`, the research note): the documents state it for the batch shops only ("a hit that can't be read or wasn't asked for", `CLAUDE.md:59`).
- A regular price at or below the price counts as no regular price and is dropped with no log line; amounts are rounded to grosze (`src/lib/services/shops/shop-offer.ts:19-37`). _Searched in docs_: the documents cover a regular price that "can't be read" (`context/foundation/test-plan.md:213`), not this case.
- An automatic match is stored before its first price, in two writes. A failed price write leaves the match without a price until the next refetch (`src/lib/services/shop-matching.ts:391-399`).
- A decision write is an insert and, on a conflict, a separate narrowed update. Its correctness rests on R-01's unique key and R-05's compare-and-swap (`src/lib/services/matches.ts:303-342`).
- A gate call spends its slot even when the shop doesn't answer (`src/lib/services/shop-gate.ts:101-153`).
- `shop_requests` rows are pruned only by the same shop's next reservation (`supabase/migrations/20260926112205_polite_shop_access.sql:72-74`).

## Unknowns and limitations

- **Documents.**
  - The PRD is amended by dated update notes rather than rewritten, so a requirement's current form is its last update; superseded notes stay in place by design and are not listed as stale.
  - `README.md` is mostly the starter template's.
  - `CLAUDE.md` describes the product as built, so its agreement with the code is weaker evidence than the PRD's.
- **The narrative sample.**
  - 22 documents were read in full: the roadmap, 13 `change.md` files and 8 plan briefs.
  - 72 were only titled: the plans, research notes, reviews and follow-ups. A term or rule that only they state is not in the evidence.
  - This synthesis searched them for its key checks and found D-01's decision (`context/archive/2026-10-02-hebe-in-comparison/plan.md:646`), the accepted direct-call risk (`context/archive/2026-09-26-polite-shop-access/plan.md:405`) and the missing-header decision (`context/archive/2026-09-27-watchlist-add-by-search/reviews/impl-review.md:120`).
- **Independence.** The harness put an older copy of `CLAUDE.md` and its imports from another checkout into the two code agents' context before they started. Neither opened or cited it, and every code finding cites code read in this worktree. Still, their reading of the code may have been primed by it.
- **Code not searched.**
  - Excluded paths: `src/dev/**`, the recorded fixtures, operator and deploy scripts, `astro.config.mjs`, `wrangler.jsonc` and CI. `scripts/check-production.mjs` was opened only for its `settings` group.
  - Two consequences: the cross-site refusal of „Dodaj”, set-password and sign-out rests on Astro's `checkOrigin`, which only route comments assert; and a Cron Trigger configured in `wrangler.jsonc` would have gone unseen, though `src/` has no handler for one.
  - The Vitest unit tests' bodies were not read.
- **Configuration outside the repository:**
  - production Auth's sign-up and password settings (Q-04);
  - the production `public.shops` rows (each shop's cap and `enabled`);
  - whether a user can obtain the publishable key and project URL, which the app keeps in server env. Every direct-call gap above (R-05, R-07, R-12, R-22, R-34, R-39, R-40) depends on it.
- **Unsettled by any person in the project:** which 30-day window each shop's declared low covers; the shops don't say (`docs/research/polish-drugstore-price-apis.md:258`).
- **The verdict's states** are defined only in code, so the glossary takes the code's definitions.
